# Everything Market Working System

Main domain: `everythingmarket.co.za`

Support/acquisition domain: `marketplacesouthafrica.co.za`

## How We Work

Everything Market grows by repeating one loop:

1. Create searchable pages.
2. Bring in sellers.
3. Make every listing easy to share.
4. Build trust.
5. Track what is growing.
6. Improve weekly.

## Domain Setup

`everythingmarket.co.za` is the main brand and canonical domain.

`marketplacesouthafrica.co.za` is used for campaigns, radio, flyers, WhatsApp and memorable search intent. It redirects into Everything Market so all authority and users collect on one site.

## Weekly Operating Rhythm

### Monday: Search Footprint

- Add or improve category, province and popular-search pages.
- Check Google Search Console.
- Submit sitemap after major page additions.
- Review top queries and build pages for promising searches.

### Tuesday: Seller Supply

- Contact sellers in Facebook and WhatsApp groups.
- Invite car dealers, phone shops, furniture shops, landlords and service providers.
- Offer free store/profile setup for early sellers.
- Encourage sellers to post at least 3-5 listings.

### Wednesday: Trust

- Verify good sellers.
- Remove poor or suspicious listings.
- Improve safety content.
- Add report/seller profile improvements.

### Thursday: Social Distribution

- Share top listings by province.
- Post deal-of-the-day content.
- Ask sellers to share their listing links.
- Repost seller stories and local bargains.

### Friday: Conversion

- Improve the post-ad flow.
- Improve listing detail pages.
- Add prompts like “Post your own free ad.”
- Review where users drop off.

### Weekend: Community Push

- Push listings into local groups.
- Promote province/category campaigns.
- Encourage marketplace sharing on WhatsApp status.

## Product Priorities

1. Listing share buttons.
2. Seller/store profile pages.
3. Listing schema for `/ad/:id`.
4. Saved searches.
5. Email or WhatsApp alerts.
6. Seller analytics.
7. Reviews and ratings.
8. Boosted listings.
9. City landing pages.
10. Business directory.

## Million User Focus

To reach 1,000,000 users, the site needs:

- Lots of indexable pages.
- Lots of real listings.
- Sellers sharing their own ads.
- Trust signals that beat Facebook Marketplace.
- Repeat visits from alerts and saved searches.
- Partnerships with local businesses.

The daily question is:

> What did we do today that creates more searchable pages, more listings, more shares, or more trust?
