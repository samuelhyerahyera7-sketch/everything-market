# Everything Market Growth Plan

Goal: become a trusted South African marketplace that can compete with established classifieds and shopping brands by building search footprint, listing supply, trust, and repeat usage.

## Positioning

Everything Market should own this promise everywhere:

> South Africa's free online marketplace for buying and selling anything locally.

Lead with:

- Free to post.
- Built for all 9 provinces.
- Safe in-app messaging.
- Verified sellers.
- Cars, property, electronics, furniture, jobs, services and fashion.

## 90-Day Priorities

1. Build indexable pages at scale.
   - Category pages: cars, property, electronics, furniture, jobs, services, fashion.
   - Province pages: Gauteng, Western Cape, KwaZulu-Natal, Eastern Cape, Limpopo, Mpumalanga, North West, Free State, Northern Cape.
   - Next: category plus province pages, for example `/province/gauteng/cars-bakkies`.

2. Grow listing supply.
   - Recruit sellers from Facebook Marketplace groups, WhatsApp community groups, local dealerships, furniture shops and repair/service providers.
   - Offer free verified seller badges for the first wave of businesses.
   - Create weekly campaigns: "Free Friday Listings", "Gauteng Car Week", "Cape Town Furniture Week".

3. Build trust.
   - Publish safety pages for buyers and sellers.
   - Show verification status clearly.
   - Add report buttons and visible moderation language.
   - Collect testimonials from early sellers.

4. Win local search.
   - Target long-tail searches: "iPhone for sale South Africa", "Toyota for sale Gauteng", "flat to rent Cape Town", "driver jobs Durban".
   - Add fresh pages for popular searches and link them from the homepage/footer.
   - Submit the sitemap in Google Search Console after every major URL expansion.

5. Create repeat visits.
   - Saved searches and email/WhatsApp alerts.
   - Weekly "best deals near you" digest.
   - Social posts featuring real listings by province.

## Content Calendar

- Monday: top deals by province.
- Tuesday: seller success story.
- Wednesday: safety/trust tip.
- Thursday: category spotlight.
- Friday: free listing push.
- Weekend: high-intent search posts, for example cars, phones, furniture and rentals.

## Measurement

Track weekly:

- Indexed pages in Google Search Console.
- Organic clicks and impressions.
- New listings posted.
- Active sellers.
- Message starts per listing.
- Signup-to-post conversion.
- Returning visitors.

## Immediate Technical Wins Already Started

- Server-rendered SEO landing pages for top categories and provinces.
- Clean internal links from the homepage SEO section.
- Expanded sitemap with category and province URLs.
- Admin security hardening so trust operations are safer.
