## Weekly Seller Targets

- 25 private sellers.
- 10 small businesses.
- 5 dealers or store owners.
- 100 new listings.
- 20 sellers sharing their own listing links.

## Seller Quality Checklist

Ask sellers to include:

- Clear title.
- Real photos.
- Price.
- City/province.
- Honest condition.
- Short description.
- Safe pickup/payment instructions.

## Follow-Up Message
